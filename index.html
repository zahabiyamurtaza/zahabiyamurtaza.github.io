R

Publications

Mama always said, “Don’t judge a book by its cover.” I was never much of a rebellious kid, quite the opposite actually, but my one quiet act of rebellion was choosing books solely by their covers. If it was pink, pretty or beautifully designed, it went straight into my basket.

Even now, as a fairly avid reader, I secretly still do it. And honestly, that instinct has shaped the way I approach magazine and book design. Every cover begins with one simple thought: would I want to be seen holding this in public? Because to me, a cover should do more than look good. It should make someone want to pick it up before they’ve read a single word.

Volunteer work

Giving back has always mattered to me, and I was lucky enough to stumble upon ALERT INDIA, an NGO working on leprosy, HIV/AIDS and TB.

With them, I’ve designed two annual reports, helped write them, and written a research paper of my own. This is work for the soul, and I hope it’s only the beginning.

Annual reports

ALERT INDIA’s yearly report to its donors and partners. I designed the covers and the inside layout, and helped with the copywriting.

Research paper

Written with the help of Mr Anthony Samy, Chief Executive of ALERT INDIA, and the books and pamphlets the organisation has built up over the years.

Research paper

Studying the Leprosy Crisis in India

In 2005, the WHO announced that leprosy had been eliminated in India. The word was soon mistaken for eradication: case detection slowed, awareness work faded and funding moved elsewhere. Today India still has more leprosy cases than any other country. This paper looks at how that happened, and what it has cost the people still living with the disease.
